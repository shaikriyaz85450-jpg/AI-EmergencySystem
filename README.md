# 🚨 AI Emergency Response Assistant

An AI-powered emergency call-centre intelligence dashboard that transforms reports from audio, WhatsApp, SMS, social media, operators, and responders into meaningful, continuously updated incidents.

Instead of making emergency operators manually compare hundreds of incoming reports, the system uses AI to extract important information, understand locations, correlate related reports, track incident updates, and help operators focus on incidents that actually require attention.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Emergency call centres receive reports from multiple sources:

- 📞 Audio calls
- 💬 WhatsApp
- 📱 SMS
- 🌐 Social media
- 👨‍🚒 Responders
- 👨‍💼 Operators

Many reports may describe the **same real-world emergency**, while others may be rumors, irrelevant questions, or unrelated emergencies.

Manually comparing these reports can cause:

- Information overload
- Delayed decision-making
- Repeated follow-up calls
- Missed important updates
- Duplicate handling of the same emergency

---

## 💡 Solution

The **AI Emergency Response Assistant** converts raw reports into meaningful incidents.

```text
